## 🏛️ Comprehensive Regional Field Guides

Verified logistical dossiers published by Things To Do Radar across 12 key regions:

### 1. Historic Rails & Winter Transit
* **New York Transit Museum Nostalgia Train (New York, NY):** Vintage 1930s R1/9 subway cars on select December Sundays. [Transit Museum Holiday Nostalgia Train Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/new-york-transit-museum-brooklyn-holiday-nostalgia-rides-guide)
* **Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad (Durango, CO):** 1882 coal-fired steam engine through Cascade Canyon. [Durango Polar Express Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/durango-polar-express-guide)
* **New York Botanical Garden Holiday Train Show (Bronx, NY):** G-scale model locomotives among 190+ plant-based miniature landmarks. [NYBG Holiday Train Show Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/new-york-botanical-garden-holiday-train-show-guide)

### 2. Grand Botanical & Riverfront Illuminations
* **A Longwood Christmas (Kennett Square, PA):** Starlight Conservatory, floating firs, and illuminated outdoor fountains. [Longwood Gardens Christmas Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/longwood-gardens-christmas-2026-guide)
* **Natchitoches Christmas Festival (Natchitoches, LA):** 300,000 sparkling riverbank lights in Louisiana's oldest settlement. [Natchitoches Cane River Festival Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/natchitoches-christmas-festival-2026-guide)
* **Niagara Falls Winter Festival of Lights (Niagara Falls, NY):** Crystalline rime ice and jewel-toned illumination across Horseshoe Falls. [Niagara Falls State Park Winter Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/niagara-falls-state-park-winter-guide)
* **Boston Common Christmas Tree Tradition (Boston, MA):** 108-year Nova Scotia gift of gratitude and Frog Pond skating. [Boston Common Christmas Tree Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/journal/boston-common-christmas-tree-2026-nova-scotia-arrival-lighting)
* **Balboa Park December Nights (San Diego, CA):** SoCal's largest free holiday festival and museum open house. [Balboa Park December Nights Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/balboa-park-december-nights-guide)

### 3. Open-Air Trade Days & Heritage Bazaars
* **First Monday Trade Days (Canton, TX):** World's largest open-air flea market covering 400 acres and 5,000 vendors. [Canton First Monday Trade Days Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/first-monday-trade-days-canton-guide)
* **Las Vegas Cowboy Christmas & NFR Gift Show (Las Vegas, NV):** 500,000 sq ft of custom Western saddlery and fine art. [Las Vegas Cowboy Christmas Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/las-vegas-cowboy-christmas-guide)
* **Chicago Christkindlmarket at Daley Plaza (Chicago, IL):** Traditional German timber huts and Glühwein boot mugs. [Chicago Christkindlmarket Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/chicago-christkindlmarket-guide)
* **Seattle Christmas Market & Winterfest (Seattle Center, WA):** Bavarian market village beneath the Space Needle. [Seattle Christmas Market Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/seattle-christmas-market-winterfest-guide)
* **Bank of America Winter Village at Bryant Park (Manhattan, NY):** 170+ custom glass kiosks and free ice skating rink. [Bryant Park Winter Village Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/bryant-park-winter-village-guide)
* **Rockefeller Center Christmas Tree & Plaza (Manhattan, NY):** World-famous Norway Spruce and Prometheus rink. [Rockefeller Center Christmas Tree Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/rockefeller-center-christmas-tree-2026-guide)
* **Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Centennial (Manhattan, NY):** 100th anniversary route from 77th St to Herald Square. [Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-2026-guide)
* **Scott Antique Market (Atlanta, GA):** America's premier indoor antique extravaganza. [Scott Antique Market Atlanta Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/scott-antique-market-atlanta-guide)
* **Nashville Flea Market (Nashville, TN):** The historic Fairgrounds market tradition. [Nashville Flea Market Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/nashville-flea-market-guide)
* **Hallmark Christmas Experience (Kansas City, MO):** Crown Center holiday spectacle. [Hallmark Christmas Experience Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/hallmark-christmas-experience-kansas-city-guide)

### 4. Coastal Flotillas & Southwestern Sanctuaries
* **Newport Beach Christmas Boat Parade (Newport Beach, CA):** 118th annual decorated yacht harbor procession. [Newport Beach Christmas Boat Parade Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/newport-beach-christmas-boat-parade-2026-guide)
* **White Sands National Park Winter Sledding (New Mexico):** Gypsum dunes sledding and sunset strolls. [White Sands National Park Guide](https://thingstodoradar.com/guides/white-sands-national-park-winter-sledding-guide)
